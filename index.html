FAQ

Asked before booking.

Do you work on PTAC and through-the-wall units?

Yes — they're a speciality. We repair, service and replace PTACs and wall-sleeve units from Amana, Friedrich, Islandaire and other makers, and we match replacements to the sleeve you already have.

Will you coordinate with my super or building management?

Yes. Tell us the building and who to contact, and we'll arrange access with the super or management office and keep them in the loop. For buildings, we can service units across the whole property in one visit.

Can you take out my window AC and store it for the winter?

Yes. In the fall we remove window units, store them over the winter and reinstall them in spring — cleaned and checked, ready for the first hot day. If you'd rather keep the unit in place, we can winterize it instead.

Will I know the price before you start?

Always. We diagnose first, explain what failed in plain English, give you the options and quote the work. Nothing starts until you approve it.

Repair or replace — how do I decide?

It comes down to the age of the unit, how often it's failing and what the repair costs against a new one. We'll price both honestly and tell you which we'd choose in your place — then it's your call.

Do you also fix refrigerators and washing machines?

Yes. We repair refrigerators, deep freezers and washing machines across the major brands. Call us or book online and tell us what's wrong.
