Chelsea HVAC Services (“we”, “us”) provides heating, cooling, refrigeration and related services in New York. This policy explains what we collect through chelseahvacservices.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • What you give us. When you book a service, request a call-back or get in touch, we collect your name, phone number, ZIP code, the service you need, and anything else you choose to tell us. If you call, text or email, we receive your number or address and the content of your message.
  • What is collected automatically. If you accept analytics cookies, we collect usage data: pages visited, device and browser, approximate city, how you arrived, and whether you clicked to call or sent a form.

How we use it

  • To answer your request, schedule service, and provide quotes and invoices.
  • To contact you about your appointment by phone, text or email.
  • To understand how the site is used so we can improve it and our advertising.
  • To keep the records that warranties, accounting and the law require.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Who else handles it

Only the providers who help us run the business, each under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Analytics measures site usage, and only if you accept cookies.
  • Vercel hosts the website and keeps standard server logs.

Cookies

Analytics cookies are set only after you choose “Accept” on our cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and Google receives only cookieless, anonymous signals, as described by Google Consent Mode. Your choice is stored in your browser so we do not ask again. Change it any time with “Cookie settings” at the foot of our home page, or by clearing your browser's site data.
